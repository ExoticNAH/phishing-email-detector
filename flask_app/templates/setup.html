📘 How to Generate a Gmail App Password

Gmail requires an App Password when external applications securely access your inbox.

  1. Enable 2-Step Verification in your Google Account
  2. Open Google Account → Security
  3. Find App Passwords
  4. Select Mail as the application
  5. Generate the 16-character password
  6. Paste the password in the field above

🔒 Privacy Notice

Your credentials are stored only in a temporary session and automatically removed after logout or inactivity (15 minutes). The system does not store your email data permanently.
